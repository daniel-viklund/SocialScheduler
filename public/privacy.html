Social Scheduler – Privacy policy

Social Scheduler is a self-hosted tool that its owner runs to schedule uploads to their own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

What it stores

The videos and images you schedule, the text that goes with them, and the OAuth tokens for the connected accounts are stored on the server that runs this app. They are used only to upload the posts you schedule, and are never shared with or sold to anyone.

Google user data

The app asks Google for the YouTube upload permission only, and uses it solely to upload the videos you schedule to your own channel. It does not read, store or share any other data from your Google account. You can revoke its access at any time from Google account permissions.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram data

The Instagram access token is used only to publish the posts you schedule to your own account, and Instagram fetches the scheduled media from this server.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
